Tata Steel Chess Tournament

This chess tournament is known as the "Wimbledon of chess", the Tata Steel Chess Tournament is one of the most prestigious events in the international chess calendar. The tournament celebrated its 87th anniversary in 2025. Right next to the Masters and Challengers, thousands of amateur chess players of all playing strengths travel to Wijk aan Zee to play next to the world's very best. Each year, the Amateur Tournaments are a vital part of the Tata Steel Chess Tournament! From practical information to insightful updates: discover everything you need to know regarding the Amateur Tournaments on our dedicated amateur website.
