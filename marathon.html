Tata Mumbai Marathon

The largest marathon in Asia, the Tata Mumbai Marathon includes a Full Marathon(42.195 kms),
Half Marathon(21.097 kms), Dream Run(6.6 km), Senior Citizens Race(4.7 km), Champions with
Disability category(2.1 km) and a newly added Open 10K run. Over 46,000 participants take to the streets
of Mumbai on the third Sunday of January every year.
